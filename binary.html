BINARY

"Hollow Men" — issue one

Single issue  ·  24 pages  ·  Full color

Genre: Sci-Fi Monster Fight

Summary

BINARY is a sci-fi monster fight comic set in a myth-soaked, post-war world of industrial ruin and creeping magic. Think Dune meets Thundercats: high-concept, high-emotion, and high-impact.

In this debut issue, two haunted soldiers, Omiros and Kos, search for a rare ingredient to save Kos's life. Their quest leads them into a decaying bridge, where a rhino-like monster has nested… and a divine, octopus-like beast believes it has been sent to destroy it. What follows is a brutal, beautiful monster brawl wrapped in themes of redemption, fate, and survival.
