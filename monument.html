MONUMENT

Genre: Suburban Mystery / Coming-of-Age Drama

Summary

High school senior Declan Sullivan just returned to Norfolk, a predominantly Irish Catholic Boston suburb, from a summer church trip to Ireland. After a summer spent gazing across the sea with his new Irish girlfriend, Kaitlyn, Declan returns home with a bold new direction and an engagement to Kaitlyn. This year he'll save his money for the wedding, bring Kaitlyn to the States, and join the Coast Guard after he graduates high school. His family thinks he's crazy, and some of his friends doubt Kaitlyn's existence.

Shortly after Declan's return, the town is shaken by the mysterious murder of one of his classmates. The murderer is still on the loose, and everyone is on edge. To make matters worse, letters revealing deep dark secrets begin appearing in mailboxes all over Norfolk — the school principal's drinking problem, a young student's real father, a priest's abuse of a child parishioner. Will a letter arrive revealing who committed the murder? The letters seem targeted to tear apart the very fabric of the community.

While the letters haunt Norfolk, its citizens try to keep living their lives. Teenagers raise hell. They fall in and out of love. Hockey games on the frozen reservoir continue. The school musical goes on, albeit as a fiasco. Parents work their jobs, trying to keep things together, knowing their quiet town could change at any moment.
