FULL COLOR

Black & white  ·  Original graphic novel  ·  Published by Image Comics  ·  170 pages

Genre: Crime Drama / Urban Noir

Summary

A lifetime marked with Napoleonic bosses has generated a rage in Boom that she can't contain anymore — only aim. Her target? Her boss.

That same day, Boom comes home to find an old friend standing on her fire escape. David's double-crossed a drug dealer and he's looking for help. FULL COLOR is an action-packed odyssey through New York.
